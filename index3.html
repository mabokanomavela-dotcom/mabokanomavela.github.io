Welcome to Lesotho

Lesotho is known for its spectacular mountain landscapes, cultural heritage and outdoor experiences.

Our tourism website introduces visitors to some of the destinations and experiences that make Lesotho a special place to explore.

From mountain adventures and scenic landscapes to historical and cultural attractions, there are many places to discover.

Tourism Highlights

Lesotho offers a combination of natural, cultural and adventure attractions.

  • Mountain landscapes
  • Maletsunyane Falls
  • Katse Dam
  • Thaba Bosiu
  • Basotho pony experiences

Did You Know?

Lesotho is often associated with its mountainous landscape and is known as the "Mountain Kingdom".
